Frequently Asked Questions

Here are a few helpful details to make planning your visit easier. If you’re unsure about anything, please get in touch with us.

Back to the wedding website

What should I wear?

Think smart, comfortable and countryside-friendly. Cornwall can be breezy, so bringing an extra layer and wearing sensible footwear is a good idea.

Can I pay by card at the bar?

Yes. The bar accepts both cash and card. Drinks tokens will also be provided as part of the evening meal arrangements.

Is the venue accessible?

The venue is very accessible. If you have a particular access requirement or would like to check a specific detail before the day, please contact us so we can help you plan.

What time does everything happen?

Guests can arrive from 2:00 pm, when the bar opens. The ceremony is at 3:00 pm, reception drinks and confetti follow at 3:30 pm, and the buffet and speeches are planned for 6:00 pm. Evening guests are due from 7:00 pm, with music and the first dance at 7:30 pm. The bar is scheduled to close at midnight. Please check the main website nearer the date for any final updates.

Is food provided?

The wedding buffet is planned for 6:00 pm. Please let us know about dietary requirements and allergies in your RSVP so we can pass the information on to the catering team. If you have a serious allergy, please contact us directly as well.

Where can I stay?

On-site accommodation is reserved for family and the bridal party. There are also hotels, B&Bs and holiday lets around St Austell, Charlestown and nearby villages. Please book early and check your route to the venue.

How do I get to the venue?

The venue is at Knightor Winery, Trethurgy, near St Austell, Cornwall, PL26 8YQ. If travelling by train, St Austell is the nearest major railway station; plan onward transport from the station to the winery in advance.
